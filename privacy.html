Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how El Fogón D' Geny ("we," "us," or "our") handles information through the public restaurant menu and the private POS Fogón D' Geny ordering, kitchen, notification, and Clover payment workflow (collectively, the "Application").

Payment-card information: Customers present or enter card information directly on authorized Clover hardware. The Application does not collect or store full card numbers, CVV security codes, PINs, or magnetic-stripe data.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how the Application is used, we may collect:

  • Customer and order information: name, telephone number, preferred language, order type, ordered items, quantities, selections, notes, totals, order status, and timestamps.
  • Payment-related records: payment method, amount, payment status, transaction or external payment identifiers, and operational timestamps. These records do not include full card data.
  • Clover authorization information: merchant identifiers, authorization status, restricted server-side OAuth credentials, expiration information, and authorization or refresh audit records needed to maintain the Clover connection.
  • Device and technical information: kiosk or terminal identifiers, device configuration, session identifiers, browser or device information, timestamps, security events, error logs, and diagnostic data.
  • Administrative information: authorized employee or device configuration, application settings, menu changes, item availability, and audit information associated with administrative actions.
  • Communications: information provided when a customer or authorized user contacts us or receives an order-status notification.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • receive, prepare, manage, and complete restaurant orders;
  • display kitchen and order status to authorized staff;
  • send requested or operational order-ready notifications;
  • initiate and reconcile card-present payment requests on authorized Clover devices;
  • manage menus, item availability, kiosks, devices, and authorized sessions;
  • prevent duplicate orders, unauthorized use, fraud, and security incidents;
  • troubleshoot errors, maintain the Application, and improve reliability; and
  • comply with legal, tax, accounting, dispute, and payment-processing obligations.

3. Clover payment processing

When staff initiates a card-present payment, the Application uses Clover OAuth and Cloud Pay Display to send the amount and related transaction request to an authorized Clover Mini or Clover Flex. Clover and its processing partners process the customer's payment-card information under their own agreements and privacy practices. We receive only the operational result and identifiers needed to associate the payment with the corresponding restaurant order.

4. When information is disclosed

We may disclose information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Clover and payment-processing partners for authorization, card-present payment processing, transaction status, security, and support;
  • Supabase and other infrastructure providers for database, authentication, serverless processing, logging, security, and application hosting functions;
  • Meta/WhatsApp when an order-ready or other requested operational notification is sent through WhatsApp;
  • authorized personnel and service providers who need access to operate, secure, support, or maintain the Application; and
  • government authorities or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, users, the business, or the integrity of the Application.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Retention

Restaurant order records are generally retained for up to six months. Some information may be retained longer when reasonably necessary for payment disputes, fraud prevention, security, accounting, legal compliance, backup cycles, or enforcement of our agreements. Clover authorization information is retained while the connection is active or until it is revoked, replaced, or no longer needed. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer reasonably required, subject to applicable obligations and technical limitations.

6. Cookies, local storage, and tracking choices

The Application may use browser storage, session identifiers, or similar technologies that are necessary to remember language, cart, kiosk, device, security, and administrative-session settings. These technologies support Application operation and are not used by us for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because we do not sell or share personal information for such advertising, browser Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals do not change the Application's essential operational data practices.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information, including access controls, restricted server-side functions, session controls, and separation of payment-card entry from the web Application. No system can guarantee absolute security. Authorized users must protect their credentials, administrative PINs, and devices and must promptly report suspected unauthorized access.

8. California privacy requests

California residents may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, subject to verification and applicable legal exceptions. A person may also ask questions about our information practices by using the contact details below. We will not unlawfully discriminate against a person for making a privacy request.

To protect personal information, we may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify the identity and authority of the requester before completing a request.

9. Children's privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Application. A parent or guardian who believes that a child has provided personal information may contact us to request review and deletion.

10. Third-party links and services

The Application may link to or rely on services operated by third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. This Privacy Policy does not control how Clover, Meta/WhatsApp, device manufacturers, internet providers, or other independent third parties handle information under their own authority.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Application, applicable requirements, or our practices change. The current version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
